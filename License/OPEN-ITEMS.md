- **Gaia:** original subset header's CC BY 4.0 assertion conflicts with current ESA data terms. Confirm the terms applicable to the original acquisition and obtain commercial authorization where needed. Current ESA archive terms direct requests to `data.licences@esa.int`. No receipt or separate agreement was supplied. Do not market this dataset as unrestricted CC BY 4.0.

- **JPL:** verify permission/terms for the actual SBDB/Horizons/kernel-derived distribution. SSD's FAQ says permission is needed to republish site information, and JPL's copyright notice is not a universal public-domain grant. We have not requested permission or concluded that every numerical fact has copyright protection; clarify the actual files/use with JPL. NASA image policy is not a data/software license.
- **Unidentified constellation data:** trace stars/names/figure line geometry/boundaries in `constellation-data.js`; do not assume they inherit the known HYG supplement's license.
- **Textures:** Earth day/night KTX2 assets at 2K/4K/8K are now mapped and preserved byte-for-byte; confirm their source-to-KTX2 conversion history. Map any other original downloads to resized/reprojected/KTX2 results. Verify the Milky Way variant from SVS4851 and independently trace starmap/overlay files. Retain third-party credits and required modification notices.
- **Dependencies:** confirm exact Astronomy Engine version; TOP2013 file; Basis WASM revision and transitive licenses; any extra THREE addon or vendor module not present in uploads.
- **Original application:** rights holder should choose/confirm its license; review externally sourced shader snippets, fonts/icons and other missing assets. No project-wide license has been selected by this patch.

Keep this folder with distributed files and provide accessible credits where license terms require them. A completed folder structure does not mean every item is cleared for commercial release.
