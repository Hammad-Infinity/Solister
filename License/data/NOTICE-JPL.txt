Identified from local metadata:
- smallbodies-meta.json: 68,155 catalog objects; SBDB query 2026-09-19, NEOWISE/albedo-derived sizes and custom Horizons DE441 resonant/secular fitting.
- kuiper-meta.json: 24 named bodies, geometric heliocentric J2000 ecliptic TDB states; selected/converted offline Hermite tables.
- Pluto/Charon: PLU060/DE440; Himalia: JUP347/DE442; Phoebe: SAT441L, plus supplied historical Horizons JSONs. Preserve per-file details rather than replacing them with one assumed solution identifier.
- Body catalog identifies NEP098 for Triton/Nereid; actual binary/kernel-generation inputs not supplied.
- V8 comet-ephemeris.json/bin and validation retain query/solution records and source-variation diagnostics. Fun motion and visible nuclei/comae/tails are illustrative project models, not mission imagery.
